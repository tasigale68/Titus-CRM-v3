Human Services · Long Day Care · Family Day Care · Outside School Hours Care · Vacation Care

You're Educating Children and Running a Compliance Operation. Titus Handles the Second One.

Child care and OSHC services operate under some of the most detailed regulatory requirements in the sector. Educator ratios. NQF Quality Areas. Incident notifications. Staff qualification tracking. Government reporting. Most services manage this across whiteboards, spreadsheets, and group chats. Titus brings it into one platform. So your educators can stay in the room with the children instead of the office doing paperwork.

See How Titus Works for Child Care

Long Day Care + OSHC

Six problems every child care service shares.

Different setting. Same compliance burden. Same paper trail that never ends.

Educator Qualification Tracking

Expiring certs across 25 educators

First aid. Working With Children Checks. Diploma currency. You find out someone has lapsed when the assessor asks for it. Titus shows colour-coded compliance badges for every educator and sends automatic expiry alerts before anything lapses.

Incident Notification and Documentation

24-hour reporting requirement

A child falls in the playground. The educator writes it on paper, the director types it up later, and the notification deadline quietly ticks past. Titus provides AI-assisted incident reporting with classification, timestamps, and audit-ready records created at the point of event.

Staff Rostering Across Ratios

Sick call at 6:45am

An educator calls in sick before opening. You need a qualified replacement who meets ratio requirements in the next hour. Titus offers smart rostering with AI-powered matching, availability checks, and bulk SMS to fill shifts fast.

Government and Funder Reporting

Quarterly KPIs across multiple programmes

Pulling numbers from three different spreadsheets for your quarterly report. Every time. Titus keeps compliance data live and lets you configure reporting fields that match your funding body requirements. Pull the report when you need it. The data is already there.

Policy and Procedure Document Control

40 overdue policies and no review schedule

Your policy register lives in a shared drive. Half the documents have not been reviewed in two years. Nobody knows which version is current. Titus provides a policy register with review date tracking, version control, and alerts when reviews are due.

After-Hours Parent Enquiries

7pm calls going to voicemail

Parents call after hours about enrolment, fees, or tomorrow's schedule. Nobody answers. They move on. Andy AI answers every call 24/7, triages the enquiry, takes a message, and routes it to the right person the next morning.

Long Day Care

Centre-based care. Centre-based compliance.

Long day care directors manage permanent facilities, fixed educator teams, and continuous quality improvement. These are the operational problems Titus was built to handle.

Real-Time Ratio Compliance

Whiteboard ratios that are wrong by 10am

Your whiteboard says you are compliant. But two educators swapped rooms, one went on break, and a casual did not show. The ratio is wrong and nobody noticed. Titus replaces the whiteboard with a live roster view that shows real-time educator-to-child ratios and flags breaches before they happen.

NQF Quality Area Evidence Collection

Scrambling for evidence before Assessment and Rating

Your assessment visit is in six weeks and you have no organised evidence for Quality Areas 4, 5, or 7. Titus captures evidence continuously as part of normal operations. When assessment time comes, generate a PDF evidence pack on demand. No scramble. No all-nighter.

Enrolment and Waitlist Management

Waitlist in a notebook, follow-ups in your head

Families enquire. You write their name in a book. You forget to follow up. They enrol somewhere else. Titus adapts its referral pipeline to an enrolment workflow with stages, automated follow-ups, and waitlist visibility so no family falls through the cracks.

Approved Provider Obligations

Multi-site oversight with no single view

Approved providers running multiple centres need visibility across all sites without logging into each one separately. Titus provides multi-site dashboards with role-based access so directors see their centre and providers see everything.

Outside School Hours Care

Multiple sites. Casual workforce. Moving targets.

OSHC coordinators run before-school, after-school, and vacation care programmes across several school sites with a largely casual workforce. Different operational profile. Same compliance framework.

Casual Workforce Coordination

Texting 15 casuals to fill tomorrow's shift

Your casual pool changes week to week. Nobody knows who is available, who is qualified for which site, or who worked last. Titus tracks availability, qualifications, and preferences, then auto-matches casuals to open shifts across your sites.

Multi-Site Programme Management

No single view across school sites

You run programmes at four schools. Each has its own folder, its own spreadsheet, its own communication channel. Titus gives you one dashboard across all sites with programme schedules, staff allocation, and attendance data in one place.

Vacation Care Planning and Staffing

School holidays planning starts too late

Vacation care needs excursion planning, capacity limits, dietary requirements, and enough qualified staff for extended hours. Titus provides task management for programme planning alongside capacity tracking and staffing tools so nothing gets missed.

Parent Communication at Scale

Permission slips lost, parents not informed

Sending individual texts to 200 families across four sites about schedule changes, excursion permissions, or closures. Titus handles bulk SMS and email with permission form tracking so you know exactly who has responded and who has not.

CCS Attendance and Booking Records

Paper sign-in sheets and manual data entry

Child Care Subsidy compliance requires accurate attendance records with timestamps. Paper sign-in sheets create double handling and errors. Titus provides digital sign-in and sign-out with timestamps that feed directly into your attendance records.
