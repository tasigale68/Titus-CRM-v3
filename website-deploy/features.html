Module 1

Core Operations

5 features

Smart Rostering & AI Suggestions

AI scores workers by distance, overtime risk, availability, and client preferences. One click assign with drag and drop. Conflict warnings for unavailable staff.

Task Manager

Create, assign, and track tasks across your team. Priority levels, due dates, status tracking, and assignment to specific staff. Tasks auto created from incidents and compliance events.

Sites & Locations

Manage all service delivery locations with addresses, contacts, and notes. Link sites to roster shifts. Track which workers service which locations.

Timesheets & Staff Hours

Workers log actual start and end times with breaks. Managers review and approve. Staff hours overview shows total hours per worker across any date range with CSV export.

Award Payroll Engine

Full modern award calculation engine. Supports SCHADS (human services), Fair Work Act, and configurable award rates. Every penalty, every allowance, every overtime rule.

Module 2

AI Powered

5 features

Andy AI Chatbot

AI assistant trained on your industry regulations, award conditions, and organisation policies. Answers questions instantly. Auto generates business and continuity plans.

AI Receptionist (Calls & SMS)

Twilio powered phone system. AI answers calls, greets known callers by name, routes or takes messages, and creates tasks automatically.

AI Voice Field Notes

Record observations in plain language. AI structures the data, classifies entries, and maps to your operational framework. Works for care notes, site reports, inspection logs.

AI Receipt Parsing

Contractors photograph receipts and AI instantly extracts vendor, amount, date, GST, ABN, and expense category. 16 expense categories with automatic GST calculation.

Bulk CV Upload & AI Assessment

Upload CVs in bulk. AI classifies the correct award level and pay point, extracts certifications and skills, and flags concerns. Supports SCHADS and other modern awards.

Contacts & Pipeline

Referral Pipeline

Unified Inbox (Email, SMS, Calls)

All communication in one place. Gmail/Outlook/IMAP integration. Link messages to contacts. Call transcripts and AI summaries.
