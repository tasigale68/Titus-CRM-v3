Why Professional Firms Switch

Eight challenges every professional services firm faces

1

Client files scattered across multiple tools

Email, Drive, CRM, billing all separate. No single source of truth for client information, contracts, and communications.

2

Staff utilisation tracked in spreadsheets

No real-time view of who's busy, who's available, or billable hours. Capacity planning is a guessing game.

3

Billing hours logged inconsistently

Revenue leakage from untracked time, forgotten entries, and manual reconciliation. No audit trail.

4

Compliance documentation is laborious

Manual registers, version control issues, and no audit logs. One missed entry = regulatory risk.

5

New client onboarding takes weeks

Contracts, KYC checks, system access all manual. Missing docs discovered after engagement starts.

6

Partner reporting takes days to prepare

No live dashboards. Manual data pulls, spreadsheet consolidation, and late-night email updates.

7

No AI to draft client communications

Reports, proposals and memos written from scratch. Hours per week in repetitive writing.

8

Multiple subscriptions draining margin

Six to eight tools, none fully integrated. $500-$2,000/month on scattered platforms eating into profit.
