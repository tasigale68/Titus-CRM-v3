Aged Care Quality Standards Compliance

Eight standards, continuous improvement evidence, and the Commission can visit without notice.

18 configurable compliance registers. Evidence captured continuously. PDF audit pack generated on demand.

Workforce Rostering Across Multiple Sites

Scheduling home care workers across 40 clients and 3 residential sites with different shift patterns.

Smart rostering with AI suggestions. Distance, availability, and qualifications factored in. Bulk shift notifications.

03

Incident Reporting and SIRS

Serious Incident Response Scheme requires reporting within 24 hours. Our current process takes three days.

AI-assisted incident classification. Severity auto-assessed. Timestamped. Stored in compliance register. Reportable incidents flagged immediately.

Staff Qualification and Training Tracking

Mandatory training requirements, police checks, flu vaccination records, manual handling certs. All on different cycles.

Colour-coded compliance badges on every staff profile. Automatic expiry alerts. LMS tracks completion.

Progress Notes and Clinical Documentation

Carers spend 45 minutes after every shift writing notes. Most of it is the same information in a different format.

Voice progress notes. AI extracts 30+ fields. Mapped to care goals. Done in 2 minutes.

Family and Stakeholder Communication

Families want updates. GPs need information. Care managers need reports. Everyone is calling the same coordinator.

Unified inbox. Email, SMS, calls in one place. Andy AI handles routine enquiries 24/7.
