Why Providers Choose Titus Over Generic CRMs

Most CRM platforms were designed for sales teams and marketing departments. Titus CRM was designed from day one for the specific workflows that NDIS, Aged Care, and Community Service providers deal with every day. Here's what makes it different:

  • SCHADS Award compliance built into rostering — penalty rates for Saturday, Sunday, public holidays, overtime, and broken shifts are calculated automatically. No spreadsheet formulas to maintain.
  • 18 QMS registers populated from daily operations — incident reports, worker screening, medication logs, complaints, and more. Six registers auto-populate from operational data, so audit preparation takes minutes instead of weeks.
  • NDIS budget tracking at the line item level — 635 support items from the 2025-26 NDIS Price Guide built in. Client budgets update in real time as shifts are rostered and completed.
  • Voice-to-text progress notes — support workers speak their notes using Australian English speech recognition. AI structures the transcript into NDIS-compliant documentation with goal references.
  • Digital service agreements with automated reminders — participants sign on their phone. Automated follow-up at 3, 7, and 12 days. Expiry reminders at 14, 7, and 3 days before plan end.
  • 24/7 AI phone agent (Andy) — answers calls, takes messages, books appointments, and escalates urgent matters. Providers never miss an after-hours call again.
