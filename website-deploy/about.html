The Story Behind Titus CRM

G

Gus

Founder

Brisbane, QLD

Titus CRM exists because Gus ran an NDIS provider — Delta Community Support — and experienced the admin pain firsthand. Every night was spent buried in spreadsheets. Five different tools to manage clients, rosters, HR, recruitment, and compliance. Calls missed after hours because there was no system to catch them.

He built Titus to solve his own problems first. A single platform that replaced the patchwork of disconnected tools, automated the repetitive tasks, and gave him back his evenings. Once it worked for Delta Community, he made it available to other providers facing the same struggle.

"I was spending every night on spreadsheets, juggling 5 different tools, and missing calls after hours. I built Titus because no one else was solving this problem properly for Australian providers."
