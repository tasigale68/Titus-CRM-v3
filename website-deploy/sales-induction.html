Section 9

Objection Handling

"$60k is too expensive"
They are comparing it to a $50/month SaaS subscription.
"This is not an off the shelf subscription. This is a custom built system for your business. Run the calculator at askyrgrandpa.com/calculator. Most businesses are spending $60k+ a year on admin wages doing what this system does automatically. You get your money back in year one."
"We already use [competitor]"
They are comfortable with what they know.
"That is great, you already see the value in systems. The question is, does your current system have a built-in phone system, an AI receptionist that answers calls at 2am, and AI rostering that finds cover automatically? Most of our clients came from ShiftCare or Brevity because they outgrew them."
"We're too small for this"
They think CRM is for big companies.
"Actually, smaller businesses benefit the most because you are wearing all the hats. This takes rostering, compliance, phone answering and invoicing off your plate so you can focus on growing."
"We don't need all those features"
Overwhelmed by the feature list.
"That is the beauty of it. We build it custom for you. You only see and use what you need. Complete the assessment tool and we will show you a version built specifically for your business."
"Our staff won't use it"
Bad experience with previous tech rollouts.
"We include training videos and onboarding tools with every build. Staff log in on their phone, it takes 2 minutes to learn. We also have an AI assistant that walks them through anything they get stuck on."
"Can I see a demo first?"
They want to see before committing.
"Absolutely, that is exactly how we work. Step one is the AI Business Assessment. Once you complete that, we build a demo version of the system customised to your business. Then we walk you through it in 30 minutes. No obligation."
"We tried a CRM before and it didn't work"
Got burnt by a bad product or implementation.
"That is exactly why we custom build it for you instead of giving you a generic product. We also handle the full setup, training, and onboarding. 28 days and you are live."
"I need to talk to my partner / board"
Stalling or genuinely need buy-in.
"Of course. How about we do the 30 minute demo together with them? That way they can see the system and ask questions directly. What day works?"
"We're not ready to change yet"
Fear of disruption.
"No pressure at all. But can I ask, how much is staying the same costing you? Run your numbers through askyrgrandpa.com/calculator. It might surprise you."
"Is our data secure?"
Privacy concerns.
"100% Australian hosted data. Enterprise grade encryption. Role based access so staff only see what they need. We work with NDIS providers who have strict compliance requirements."
"What about training?"
Worried about the learning curve.
"Full training videos and onboarding tools included with every build. Plus ongoing support at $500/week. Your team will be up and running in days, not weeks."
"Does it integrate with [X]?"
Worried about their existing tools.
"Because we custom build for each business, we can integrate with whatever you need. That is part of the scoping process. Tell us what you use and we will map it out."
"We just use spreadsheets"
They think spreadsheets work fine.
"Spreadsheets cannot answer your phone at 2am, find a replacement worker by SMS, or auto generate an invoice. Run the staffing calculator. I guarantee you are spending more on admin time than you think."
"Send me some info and I'll have a look"
Polite brush off.
"Happy to. But honestly, info sheets do not do it justice. The best thing I can send you is the AI Business Assessment. Takes 5 minutes to complete and we will build a demo based on your answers. No obligation. Would you be open to that?"
