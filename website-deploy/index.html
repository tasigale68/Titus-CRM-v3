What is Titus CRM?

Titus CRM is an AI-powered operations platform built for compliance-heavy businesses in Australia and New Zealand. It replaces spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual processes with one system that handles rostering, staff management, compliance registers, document control, and a 24/7 AI receptionist. Built by A4 inside a real NDIS business (Delta Community Support), it reduced admin staff from 14 to 1 and saved over $900,000 per year in wages. Now available to every compliance-heavy business across human services, aged care, real estate, accounting, recruitment, and more.
