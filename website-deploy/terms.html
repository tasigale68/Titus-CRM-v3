7. GPS and Location Data

Important: By using the Platform, all Users agree to the collection and use of GPS and location data as described in this section. Users who do not consent to GPS data collection must not use the Platform's mobile or shift-tracking features.

7.1 What GPS Data is Collected

  • The Platform collects GPS location data from User devices during active shifts when shift tracking is enabled.
  • GPS data includes coordinates, timestamps, and movement between service delivery locations.
  • GPS tracking activates when a shift starts and deactivates when the shift ends.
  • Kilometre calculations for travel reimbursement are derived from GPS data.

7.2 Purpose of GPS Data Collection

GPS data is collected for the following purposes:

  • Worker safety: Lone worker protection and emergency location identification.
  • Service verification: Confirming service delivery at the correct location and time for NDIS billing compliance.
  • Travel recording: Automatic kilometre tracking for mileage reimbursement at the ATO rate.
  • Rostering optimisation: AI-powered staff suggestions based on proximity to Participants.
  • Audit compliance: Evidence of service delivery location for NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission audits.

7.3 Consent and Obligations

  • By accepting these Terms, Users consent to GPS data collection during active shifts.
  • Customers must inform all Users (including Support Workers, Independent Contractors, and other field staff) that GPS data will be collected during shifts prior to their first use of the Platform.
  • GPS data is not collected outside of active shift periods.
  • Users must not deliberately interfere with, spoof, or disable GPS tracking during active shifts.

7.4 Data Retention and Access

  • GPS data is retained for the duration required by NDIS record-keeping obligations (minimum 7 years).
  • GPS data may be accessed by authorised management Users (Directors, Team Leaders, Roster Officers) for rostering, safety, and compliance purposes.
  • Individual Users may view their own GPS and travel data through the Platform.
