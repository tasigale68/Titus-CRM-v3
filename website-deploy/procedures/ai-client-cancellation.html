Client Shift Cancellation Procedure

How Titus AI handles cancellation requests from clients, support coordinators, and family members across SMS, email, and phone calls.

Effective 9 April 2026 | Version 1.0
The 48-Hour Rule
Two different outcomes depending on when the client cancels relative to the shift start time.
Short Notice (Under 48 Hours)

Client cancels within 48 hours of shift start

  • Client is still charged for the full shift
  • Worker is paid minimum 2 hours at their normal hourly rate (or actual shift length if the shift was under 2 hours)
  • Shift marked as "Client Cancelled" on roster
  • Worker keeps that time slot (not freed for other shifts)
  • Counts toward billing and payroll
Standard Notice (48+ Hours)

Client cancels 48 hours or more before shift start

  • Client is NOT charged
  • Worker is NOT paid for the shift
  • Worker automatically freed for other shifts
  • Worker notified by SMS and email automatically
  • Shift stays on roster as "Client Cancelled" for records
  • Does not count toward billing or payroll

Shifts Cannot Be Deleted

Shifts must be cancelled, never deleted. This ensures a full audit trail for NDIS compliance, billing accuracy, and pattern tracking. The "Remove Shift" button has been removed from the roster.

How AI Detects Client Cancellations
Titus AI scans all inbound communication channels every 15 minutes.

Who Can Request a Cancellation?

The client themselves, a family member, a nominee or public guardian, or their support coordinator. AI identifies the requester and links them to the correct client and shift.

1

Message Received Auto

A client, family member, or support coordinator sends an SMS, email, or makes a phone call requesting to cancel a shift. Examples: "Mum won't need support tomorrow", "Cancel Thursday's visit", "We don't need anyone this weekend".

2

AI Analyses Intent Auto

Titus AI reads the message and determines: (1) Is this a client cancellation? (2) Which client? (3) Which shift date? (4) What reason? AI distinguishes client cancellations from worker cancellations and general enquiries.

3

48-Hour Rule Applied Auto

AI calculates hours between now and the shift start time. If under 48 hours: short notice rules apply. If 48+ hours: standard notice rules apply. The correct billing and payroll flags are set automatically.

Short Notice Flow (Under 48 Hours)
4

Shift Marked "Client Cancelled" Auto

Shift status changes to "Client Cancelled" with a note that it's short notice and still billable. The worker remains rostered (they were available and the client is being charged).

5

Enquiry Card Created Auto

An URGENT "CLIENT CANCELLATION" card appears in the Inbox for office staff to review. It shows the client name, shift details, reason, and confirms billing will apply.

6

Contact History Updated Auto

The cancellation is logged against both the client's and the requester's contact history for pattern tracking and reporting.

Standard Notice Flow (48+ Hours)
4

Shift Marked "Client Cancelled" and Worker Freed Auto

Shift status changes to "Client Cancelled" with a note that it's not billable. The worker is unassigned from the shift and freed for other shifts in the same time slot.

5

Worker Notified by SMS and Email Auto

The rostered worker receives an automatic SMS and email: "Hi [First Name], we've just received notification from [Client Name] that they no longer need the shift on [date] [time]. You won't be required but you can look to see if other shifts are available."

6

Enquiry Card Created Auto

A "Client Cancelled Shift" card appears in the Inbox. It confirms the client will not be charged and the worker has been notified and freed.

7

Contact History Updated Auto

Logged against the client's contact history, the requester's history (if different from client), and the worker's history showing they were notified and freed.

Key Points

What Staff Need to Know

Client cancellations are handled differently from worker cancellations. The 48-hour rule determines billing and pay. Shifts are never deleted from the system. All cancellations are permanently recorded against the client's profile. Workers are automatically notified when they're freed from a cancelled shift.

AI Is Not Perfect

Ambiguous messages may not trigger automatic detection. If a client calls and the message isn't clear, it will appear as a normal enquiry for office staff to handle manually. Always check the Inbox for any missed cancellations.
