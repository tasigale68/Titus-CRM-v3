Stakeholder Portal Guide

How to set up, manage, and support external portal access for support coordinators, guardians, nominees, and clients.

Effective 10 April 2026 | Version 1.0
Who Gets Portal Access

Support Coordinators

  • See all clients they are linked to as a Stakeholder
  • View client details, NDIS plans, rosters, service records
  • Access signed-off progress notes, incidents, behaviour records
  • View documents: NDIS plans, service agreements, reports
  • Read-only access (cannot edit any records)

Guardians, Nominees, Family

  • Same access as Support Coordinators
  • See only the clients they are linked to
  • View upcoming roster and shift details
  • Access relevant documents shared by the provider
  • Read-only access (cannot edit any records)

Clients / Participants

  • See their own information only
  • View their own roster, shifts, and worker details
  • Access their NDIS plan and budget summary
  • Read signed-off progress notes about their care
  • Read-only access (cannot edit any records)

What They Cannot See

  • Other clients not linked to them
  • Staff profiles, internal contacts, or HR records
  • Draft progress notes (only signed-off)
  • Internal documents, incidents not linked to their client
  • Company settings, audit logs, financial data
Setting Up a New Portal User
Follow these steps to create access for a new external user.
1

Verify Stakeholder Record Office Staff

Go to Contacts > Stakeholders. Ensure the person has a record with a valid email address and is linked to the correct client(s).

2

Create Portal Account Office Staff

Ask admin to create the account (via Settings or script). A unique password is generated. The account is set to the correct role (Support Coordinator or Nominee/Guardian).

3

Send Invitation Email Office Staff

Click "Send Portal Invitation" on the stakeholder's contact record. This sends a branded email with login URL, temporary password, and feature overview.

4

User Signs NDA External User

On first login, the user is shown the Platform Agreement (NDA). They must accept before accessing any data. The acceptance is timestamped and recorded.

5

Access Granted Automatic

User sees their Clients list and Roster. All data is automatically filtered to only the clients they are linked to. NDA status is visible in their contact record.

Care Passport

What is the Care Passport?

Every participant in Titus CRM is assigned a unique Care Passport ID, a portable digital identifier that stays with them throughout their care journey. This ID links all of a participant's records, ensuring continuity of care regardless of which team members are supporting them or how services change over time.

  • A single unique reference number across all providers using Titus
  • Seamless continuity when transitioning between support providers
  • All service records, progress notes, and care history linked to one ID
  • NDIS plan details, goals, and budget tracking in one place
  • Faster onboarding when a new provider joins the support team
  • Complete audit trail of all care delivered against the participant

For stakeholders: The Care Passport ID means you can track your client's complete service history, progress notes, and NDIS goal progress across their entire care journey in one place, even if they receive support from multiple teams within the organisation.

Managing Portal Users

Resetting Passwords

Directors, Team Leaders, and Roster Officers can reset any stakeholder's password from their contact record. Go to Contacts > Stakeholders > select the person > Portal Access section > "Reset Password". The new password is shown on screen for you to share securely.

NDA Tracking

Each stakeholder's NDA acceptance status is shown in their contact record under Portal Access. If the NDA version is updated, users will be prompted to re-accept on their next login.

Removing Access

To revoke access, change the user's status to Inactive in the users table or contact admin. The user will be unable to log in but their records and NDA acceptance remain for audit purposes.
