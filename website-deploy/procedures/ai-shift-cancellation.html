AI Shift Cancellation Detection

How Titus AI automatically detects when staff cancel shifts and what office staff need to do about it.

Effective 9 April 2026 · Office Procedures Document

What Happens (Step by Step)
The full process from staff message to shift reassignment.
Step 1 · AI Detection
Staff sends cancellation message
A support worker texts, emails, or calls to say they can't make their shift. They might say "I'm not feeling well", "can't come in tomorrow", "family emergency", etc.
Step 2 · AI Detection
AI reads the message and identifies cancellation intent
Titus AI analyses the message content and determines if it's a shift cancellation. It extracts the reason (Sick, Personal, Family Emergency, No Transport, No Show, Other) and identifies which shift is affected.
Step 3 · Automatic
Shift moved to VACANT
The worker is automatically unassigned from the shift. The shift stays on the roster but is marked as vacant and available for reassignment. The cancellation reason, notice period, and worker details are all logged.
Step 4 · Urgent Alert
URGENT notification sent to office staff
Three things happen simultaneously:
1. Push notification appears on your device (if Titus notifications are enabled)
2. SMS alert sent to Roster Officers, Team Leaders, and Directors
3. URGENT enquiry appears in the Inbox Enquiries tab (red card)
Step 5 · Your Action Required
Open the Inbox and review the URGENT card
Go to Inbox > Enquiries tab. You'll see a red card marked "SHIFT CANCELLATION" with the worker name, shift details, client, reason, and notice period.
Step 6 · Your Action Required
Reassign the shift
Click "Reassign Shift" to go directly to the Roster for that date. Find an available worker and assign them to the vacant shift. If you need to check availability, use the worker's profile or the availability view.
Step 7 · Your Action Required
Mark the enquiry as actioned
Go back to the URGENT card in the Inbox, click "Take Action", write what you did (e.g. "Reassigned to Jane Smith"), select the method, and click "Save & Close".
Complete
Cancellation is fully logged
The cancellation is recorded against the staff member's profile under Contact History > Cancelled Shifts. This tracks their total cancellations, reasons, and notice periods over time.

Who Does What
Responsibilities by role when a cancellation is detected.
Role Responsibility
Roster Officer Primary responder. Receives SMS + push alert. Opens the URGENT card, clicks "Reassign Shift", finds replacement worker, assigns them. Marks enquiry as actioned.
Team Leader Backup responder. Receives SMS + push alert. If Roster Officer hasn't actioned within 30 minutes, take over and reassign the shift. Escalate to Director if no replacement available.
Director Oversight. Receives push alert. Monitor that cancellations are being actioned promptly. Review staff Cancelled Shifts tab periodically for patterns (frequent cancellations, short notice).
Office Admin Can view URGENT cards in Inbox but does not receive SMS alerts. Assist with finding replacement workers if asked by Roster Officer or Team Leader.
Important Notes
Things to keep in mind about this feature.
The shift is NOT deleted. When AI detects a cancellation, the shift stays on the roster as a vacant slot. The client still needs care. Your job is to find a replacement worker and assign them.
AI is not perfect. If you see a cancellation that doesn't look right (e.g. a worker was just asking a question, not actually cancelling), you can dismiss the enquiry and manually re-assign the original worker to the shift.
Everything is logged. The cancellation reason, notice period, and AI detection source are all recorded against the staff member's profile. Go to Contacts > Staff > [Name] > Contact History > Cancelled Shifts to see a worker's full cancellation history.
Enable push notifications. To receive instant alerts on your device, make sure you've allowed notifications for Titus CRM in your browser. Go to Settings > Notifications and click "Enable Push Notifications".
Common Questions
Answers to questions office staff might have.
What if a worker texts something ambiguous like "I might not make it"?
The AI only acts on clear cancellation signals. Ambiguous messages will appear as a normal enquiry (amber, not red) for you to follow up on manually.
What if AI cancels the wrong shift?
Go to the Roster, find the vacant shift, and re-assign the original worker. Then dismiss the URGENT enquiry. The AI matched the worker's next scheduled shift, but if they meant a different one, you can correct it manually.
Does the worker get a confirmation that their shift was cancelled?
Not automatically. The AI detects and processes the cancellation, but it's up to you to confirm with the worker via SMS, email, or phone that their shift has been noted and a replacement is being arranged.
What cancellation reasons does AI detect?
Sick, Personal, Family Emergency, No Transport, No Show, and Other. The AI categorises the reason based on what the worker said in their message.
Can I see a worker's cancellation history?
Yes. Go to Contacts > Staff > [Name] > Contact History > Cancelled Shifts. You'll see total cancellations, last 30 days count, top reason, and a full list with dates, clients, reasons, and notice periods.
What if nobody is available to cover the shift?
Mark the enquiry as actioned with a note like "No replacement available, client notified" and contact the client or their family to let them know. Escalate to Director if needed.
