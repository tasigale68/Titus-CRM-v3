Client File Management

Files in folders. Emails in inboxes. Notes in spreadsheets.

Client records live across three or four systems. Nobody knows which version is current. Context gets lost between handovers.

Centralised client records with full contact history. One profile per client. Every interaction logged.

Staff Deadline Tracking

A shared calendar nobody checks.

BAS deadlines, lodgement dates, and review cycles tracked in a shared Google Calendar or whiteboard. Things slip. Partners find out late.

Task manager with priorities, due dates, and auto reminders. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Onboarding New Staff

Six weeks of shadowing before they're useful.

New hires learn by watching. There's no structured training. It takes weeks before they can work independently. Senior staff lose billable hours mentoring.

LMS with AI course builder. Upload your SOPs and procedures. Generate training courses automatically.

Professional Obligation Compliance

CPD hours, licence renewals, PI insurance.

Professional development logs sit in personal folders. PI insurance renewal dates are tracked in someone's head. Licence lapses get caught at audit time.

Cert tracking with colour-coded badges and auto expiry alerts. Green, amber, red. At a glance.
